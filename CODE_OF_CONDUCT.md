# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Artha respectful, inclusive and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience
or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect personal and financial information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers may edit, reject
or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents
the project.
